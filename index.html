Heart Rate Zones

Target training zones Z1–Z5 — train the right energy system for the adaptation you want.

Tanaka formula: max HR = 208 − 0.68 × age. Know your true max from a field test? Override it. Resting HR is best measured lying down, first thing in the morning.

Enter your age (and resting HR for Karvonen) to get your max heart rate and five colour-coded training zones.
