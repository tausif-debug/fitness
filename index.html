Water Intake

Daily hydration target from your weight and training — in litres. Weight in kg.

Base rule: ~33 ml per kg of body weight. Training adds ~12 ml per minute (≈ 350 ml per 30-min session).

Enter your weight to get your daily water target — in litres, glasses (250 ml) and bottles (500 ml).
